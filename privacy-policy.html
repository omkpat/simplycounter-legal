Privacy Policy — Simply Counter

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Simply Counter is a mantra and tally counter app. It is built to be simple and private: there are no ads, no accounts, and no tracking.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • It does not require you to create an account or sign in.

Data stored on your device

Your counters, counts, and history are stored only on your device, using Android's local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it.

Backup export / import

If you use the "Export backup" feature, the app creates a JSON file on your device and hands it to the share sheet you choose (e.g. email, cloud storage, messaging app) — that transfer is between you and the app you pick; Simply Counter itself does not send this file anywhere. "Import backup" reads a file you select back into the app.

Notifications

If you enable the optional daily reminder, it is scheduled and shown entirely on your device (via Android's WorkManager). No reminder data is sent to any server.

Optional support purchases ("tip jar")

Simply Counter offers optional, one-time purchases to support development, processed entirely through Google Play's in-app billing. Simply Counter does not receive or store your payment details — that information is handled by Google Play under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. A tip does not unlock any feature and no purchase history is retained by the app.

Permissions

  • Notifications — used only for the optional daily reminder you configure.
  • Internet — used only by Google Play Billing to process optional support purchases.

Children's privacy

Simply Counter does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "last updated" date.
